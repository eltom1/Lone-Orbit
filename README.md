# Lone-Orbit (orbita solitaria) (nombre temporar)

Lone Orbit is a science fiction survival game that explores loneliness, sacrifice, and the need to keep a dying spaceship alive while on a mission to save Earth from a drifting black hole. Unlike most space survival games, it focuses on emotional resilience rather than action, exploring decades of isolation, routine, and the weight of a mission whose outcome may never be known.
genre: Narrative Survival , Sci-Fi , Single Player

### What the player does
You play as Mingo, an aging astronaut alone in deep space.  
You manage a deteriorating ship, perform maintenance, go on spacewalks, and make decisions under pressure while dealing with isolation.

### Core mechanics
- Ship maintenance (oxygen, energy, structural integrity)
- Resource management (fuel, oxygen, materials)
- EVA (spacewalk) with tether and limited propulsion
- System repairs after random events (e.g., asteroid impacts)
- Farming / food production in space
- Dialogue with an AI companion
- Narrative choices (risk vs safety, action vs inaction)
- Gradual system decay over time
